These raw AAC access units encode a synthetic 440 Hz stereo tone, 44.1 kHz, using FDK AAC 2.0.3. The encoder used AOT 39 (AAC-ELD), 128 kbit/s, TT_MP4_RAW, SBR off and 480 or 512 samples per frame. The reported AudioSpecificConfig was f8e85000 or f8e84000 respectively, matching the production AirPlay decoder. Each hex line is one access unit. Only audio generated from the synthetic tone is included.
