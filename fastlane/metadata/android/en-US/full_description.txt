LiteCast turns an Android TV into a casting receiver without accounts, ads or time limits.

<b>Cast from</b>
<ul>
<li>iPhone, iPad and Mac: screen mirroring, AirPlay video and AirPlay music</li>
<li>The TV / cast button of video apps on Android and iOS (DLNA)</li>
</ul>

<b>Made for the remote</b>
<ul>
<li>Waits quietly in the background and starts with the TV</li>
<li>The player pops up when a cast arrives and closes itself when it ends</li>
<li>Seek with left/right (hold to go faster), OK to pause, Back twice to end mirroring</li>
</ul>

AirPlay is based on jqssun/android-airplay-server and UxPlay; the DLNA renderer comes from puppy-store-cast. DRM-protected content and proprietary casting protocols are not supported.
